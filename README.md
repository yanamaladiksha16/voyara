# Voyara

## AI-Powered Group Travel Platform

Voyara is an AI-powered group travel platform designed to help travelers discover compatible travel companions, create travel groups, communicate, stay connected, and travel more safely.

## The Problem

Finding compatible people to travel with can be difficult. Travelers may share a destination but have very different interests, budgets, travel styles, and expectations.

Voyara focuses on compatibility rather than simply matching travelers based on destination.

## How Voyara Works

1. **Onboarding** — Travelers create their profile and share their interests and travel preferences.
2. **Trip Creation** — Users select their destination, dates, budget, and trip requirements.
3. **VoyAi Matching** — VoyAi analyzes traveler preferences and generates compatible traveler recommendations.
4. **Traveler Discovery** — Users can explore recommended traveler profiles and connect with potential companions.
5. **Travel Tribe** — Compatible travelers can create a group and coordinate their journey together.
6. **Communication** — Group chat and notifications keep travelers connected.
7. **Trip Tracking** — Travelers can stay connected during their journey.
8. **SOS Safety Layer** — Voyara provides an SOS feature for situations where assistance may be needed.

## Core Features

* AI-powered traveler matching
* Personalized travel profiles
* Trip creation and preferences
* Traveler discovery
* Travel tribe creation
* Group communication
* Notifications
* Trip tracking
* SOS safety feature

## Technology

The current prototype is implemented using a React/TypeScript interface.

## Prototype

The project includes the Voyara user interface and demonstrates the complete intended user workflow from onboarding and trip creation through AI matching, group formation, communication, tracking, and SOS.

## Project Goal

Voyara aims to make group travel more compatible, connected, and safer by helping people find the right companions for their journey.
